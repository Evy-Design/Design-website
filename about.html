I’m a Graphic Designer, always chasing what’s next. I’m driven by new challenges and staying ahead of trends, and I’m currently a nominated graphic designer with two degrees in the field and over 5 years of agency experience.

I am a creative with two degrees in Graphic and Communication Design. I began my design journey at Grafisch Lyceum Rotterdam in the Netherlands, where I studied Corporate Design and was selected for the Masterclass as one of the top students in my first year. I then earned a Bachelor’s in Graphic Communication from The University of Northampton in the UK. I concluded my studies with a nomination for The Penguin Book Cover Design Award 2022.

I have several years of agency experience as a brand designer, creating and maintaining comprehensive visual identities from logo design and typography to UX design and motion graphics. I’ve collaborated with various creative professionals on projects for major companies like Microsoft and Shell, as well as startups and smaller businesses. Today, I work at a digital agency, where my focus is mainly on digital design.
